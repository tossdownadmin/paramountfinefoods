## Source limitations

- The official locator is search-driven and the site exposes older, concept-specific, international, and historical pages. The build must not infer a current chain-wide store count from search results.
- Prices shown in older PDF menus are not treated as current prices. Dashboard prices will be synthetic unless a future source review explicitly authorizes otherwise.
- All other store names in the synthetic dataset will be clearly fictional demo entities and will not be presented as real Paramount locations.
